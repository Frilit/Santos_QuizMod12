Bad UI Showcase: Yale School of Art

An example of how design chaos will hurt usability

Why This UI Is Bad

The Yale School of Art website, while creative in spirit, demonstrates several serious usability and design flaws:

  • Overwhelming Visual Clutter: Too many colors, boxes, and overlapping elements make it difficult to focus.
  • Poor Readability: Low contrast and inconsistent font sizes cause text to blend into the background.
  • Lack of Hierarchy: Every element competes for attention, with no clear indication of what’s important.
  • Disorganized Layout: The grid and content sections appear randomly placed, breaking the natural reading flow.

How It Could Be Improved

  • Use a limited, harmonious color palette and consistent typography.
  • Establish a clear visual hierarchy using font weights and spacing.
  • Adopt a simple grid layout for readability and user focus.
  • Keep decorative elements minimal — form should follow function.
